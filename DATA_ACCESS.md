# Data access

This repository does **not** redistribute raw ThermVision-DB media.

ThermVision-DB is available from the dataset authors through Hugging Face Datasets with the persistent identifier:

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.57967/hf/7026
- Dataset: https://huggingface.co/datasets/MAli-Farooq/ThermVision-DB
- Dataset article: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dib.2026.112506

Users must follow the access conditions and licensing terms stated by the upstream dataset repository at the time of access.

Pretrained ArcFace/AdaFace model weights are also **not** redistributed here. Their exact model identities and cryptographic hashes are recorded in the provenance files and manuscript supplementary record.
